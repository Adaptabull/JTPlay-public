JTPlay Privacy Policy

Windows Build 172. Publisher JT1527. Prepared November 6, 2026.

JTPlay uses your account identifiers, authorization tokens, server addresses, library metadata, playback activity and preferences to connect to the services you choose, display your libraries and play your media.

Plex handles Plex sign-in and your authorized server handles library and playback requests. Optional MDBList connects to its authorization and discovery services. Optional Seerr uses the server you configure for search and discovery, and sends a media request when you choose to submit one. Artwork and trailers may contact third-party providers. Trailers may use WebView2, which can store cookies separately. Connected services and Microsoft handle data under their own policies.

JTPlay stores settings, tokens, databases, cached artwork, playback state and optional downloads on your device. Some credentials are encrypted using a key stored in local preferences. Protect access to your Windows account and backups. Local logs can contain service or account details.

In Build 172, tracker synchronization, Trakt connections, inherited sign-in relays, automatic updating, telemetry and crash uploads are disabled or unconfigured. JTPlay has no hosted analytics or advertising service configured for this build.

You can disconnect integrations, remove downloads and manage local app data. For revoking access or deleting records held by a connected service, use that service’s account controls. Separately chosen download folders may remain after uninstalling.

For support or privacy questions, email support@jtmedia.win. Before sharing diagnostic information, remove passwords, tokens, account details and private server addresses.
