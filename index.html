JTPlay

Your Plex library. A better way to explore it.

JTPlay is a free Windows media player built for your personal Plex library.

Browse your movies and TV shows, pick up where you left off, and enjoy native playback with audio and subtitle controls.

Discover more. Watch more.

Explore movies and shows with optional MDBList integration. Connect your own Seerr server to discover and request new content.

Your media stays yours. JTPlay connects to your authorized Plex server.

Free. Open source. Independent.

JTPlay is derived from Plezy and licensed under GPLv3. It is independently developed and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Plex or the original Plezy maintainers.
